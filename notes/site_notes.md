# Working notes for the WIS zinc co-solvent site

- Kept the conference framing, but the site should feel larger than the PDF.
- Pull out the big anchors early: 108 formulations, 63 papers, 23 features, >20 co-solvents.
- Lifespan and conductivity are the only useful targets right now.
- CE and ESW still matter for discussion, but the site should be honest that the models are not predictive there.
- DMSO / DMF / TEP need comparison cards because they are the obvious follow-up set.
- NMP is interesting but should stay flagged as extrapolative.
